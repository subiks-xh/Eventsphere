# EventSphere - User Guide

Welcome to EventSphere, the modern event management platform.

## Getting Started

1. **Registration:**
   - Navigate to `/auth/register`.
   - Provide your details and select a Role (e.g., Organizer, Attendee).
2. **Login:**
   - Navigate to `/auth/login` and log in with your credentials.

## For Organizers

### 1. Creating Events
Go to your **Dashboard** -> **My Events** -> **Create Event**.
Fill in the details. The system will dynamically forecast the expected attendance based on historical data when you enter a Capacity and Category.

### 2. Managing Budget & Expenses
In the Event Details page, click **Budget Dashboard**.
- Set the total allocated budget.
- Log expenses.
- Expenses under ₹50,000 are auto-approved.
- Expenses over ₹50,000 require manual approval via the **Approvals Inbox**.
- Click **Export CSV** to download the financial breakdown.

### 3. Check-In & Ticketing
Use the **Check-in Attendees** page to enter Ticket IDs when guests arrive. This automatically issues a Notification to the attendee and updates live dashboard metrics.

## For Attendees

### 1. Finding & Registering for Events
Navigate to **Discover Events** to find upcoming events. Click **Register** to secure your spot.

### 2. Tickets & Certificates
- Your **My Tickets** page shows all active tickets and QR codes.
- Once you check-in at an event and the event finishes, you can download a **Certificate of Attendance** in PDF format.
